Welcome to Ancient Egypt TDG!

🎯 How to Play:

  • Place Plants: Click on a plant card, then click on a grid square to place it
  • Defend: Plants automatically shoot at enemies coming from the right
  • Earn Money: Defeat enemies to earn cash for more plants
  • Survive: Don't let enemies reach the left side or you'll lose HP!

⚠️ Watch Out For:

  • Bosses: Powerful enemies spawn after 4 minutes
  • Different Enemy Types: Some enemies are tougher or have special abilities
  • Limited Resources: Manage your money wisely!

💡 Tips:

  • Start with cheap plants like Sunflowers to earn money
  • Place Peashooters in the front rows to stop enemies early
  • Use special plants like Cactus and Thunder Pea for tough enemies
  • You have 15 seconds before enemies start spawning - use it wisely!

🌱 Plants & Abilities:

  • Sunflower (50💰): Generates 25 gold every 5 seconds
  • Peashooter (100💰): Shoots peas every 1 second
  • Cactus (150💰): Shoots spikes, deals 2 damage per hit
  • Thunder Pea (175💰): Shoots every 10 seconds, stuns enemies for 3 seconds
  • Fire Peashooter (200💰): Shoots fire peas that deal damage over time
  • Snow Pea (125💰): Shoots every 3 seconds, slows enemies for 3 seconds
  • Starfruit (75💰): Shoots in 5 directions
  • Mushroom (75💰): Generates 5 gold every 2 seconds (after 10s delay)
  • Coconut Cannon (150💰): Explosive shots every 5 seconds, area damage
  • Chomper (200💰): Eats enemies whole, 20 second cooldown
  • Spikychomper (300💰): Dragon form, shoots strong fire every 2 seconds
  • Pumpkin (150💰): Close-range attacker, targets within 2 squares
  • Flowerplant (175💰): Shoots projectiles every 1 second
  • Fireplant (200💰): Shoots fire projectiles every 1 second

👹 Enemy Types:

  • Regular Mummy: 5 HP, standard speed
  • Tough Enemy: 9 HP, slower but more durable
  • Summoner: 6 HP, spawns small enemies when killed
  • Different Enemy: 6 HP, unique appearance
  • Small Enemy: 2 HP, very fast
  • Slow Enemy: 4 HP initially, respawns with 2 HP if not fully killed
  • Ingen: Special enemy that spawns after 4 minutes, 100 HP

👑 Bosses:

  • Level 1 Boss: Thoth-like boss, 30 HP, respawns once with 100 HP
  • Level 2 Boss: Damaged Bear Animatronic, 120 HP, respawns once with 100 HP
  • Level 3 Boss: Image-based boss, 8 rows tall, 200 HP, respawns once with 200 HP, throws random images
  • Level 4 Boss: Dark purple character with binary code effects, 167 HP, respawns once with 167 HP, throws star projectiles that one-shot plants and deal 2 damage to player

🎮 Game Mechanics:

  • Money System: Start with 200💰 (Level 1), 350💰 (Level 2), 600💰 (Level 3), 800💰 (Level 4)
  • Player HP: Start with 10 HP, lose 1 HP when enemies reach left side
  • Boss Timer: Bosses spawn after 4 minutes (4:00 for levels 1, 3, 4) or 4:50 (Level 2)
  • Countdown: 15 seconds to prepare before enemies spawn
  • Level Progression: Beat the boss to advance to next level
  • Boss Respawn: Bosses respawn once after being defeated
